# WabiCare Go-to-Market Strategy: 0 to 1 Launch

**Document Owner**: Chief Product Officer + Head of Sales  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: January 2025  
**Target**: 3-5 paying customers in 90 days

---

## Executive Summary

**GTM Approach**: Land-and-Expand with Intensive Pilot Program

**Strategy**: 
1. Recruit 5 pilot clinics (free 90-day trial)
2. Deliver measurable ROI (hours saved, claims approved)
3. Convert 80%+ to paying customers
4. Use success stories to scale

**Key Differentiator**: End-to-end workflow integration (Intake → Schedule → Data → Billing) that no competitor offers.

---

## 1. Target Market Segmentation

### Primary Segment: Small to Mid-Size ABA Clinics

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**:
- **Size**: 5-20 therapists, 30-100 patients
- **Technology**: Using Microsoft 365
- **Current Tools**: Manual or basic (Excel, paper, simple scheduling)
- **Pain Points**: 
  - Intake takes 2+ weeks
  - Scheduling conflicts waste time
  - Billing errors cause revenue loss
  - No integrated system
- **Budget**: $500-1,000/month for practice management
- **Decision Maker**: Practice Owner/Administrator
- **Geography**: US-based, accessible for support

**Market Size**:
- 50,000+ ABA practices in US
- 15,000+ fit our ICP (5-20 therapists)
- Addressable market: $75M+ annually

### Secondary Segment: School Districts (Phase 2)

**Profile**:
- 10-50 therapists across multiple schools
- IEP compliance requirements
- FERPA compliance needs
- Budget: $1,000-3,000/month

## 2. Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1: Pilot Recruitment (Weeks 1-4)

#### Target: 5 Pilot Clinics

**Recruitment Channels**:

1. **LinkedIn Direct Outreach** (Primary)

   - Message: Personalized, problem-focused
   - Offer: Free 90-day trial + white-glove onboarding

2. **Industry Associations**

   - State ABA associations
   - **Goal**: 5-10 warm leads

3. **Referrals**
   - Existing network
   - Advisors and consultants
   - **Goal**: 3-5 referrals

4. **Content Marketing**
   - Blog: "How to reduce intake time from 2 weeks to 5 days"
   - Webinar: "AI in ABA therapy: Real-world results"
   - **Goal**: 5-10 inbound leads

- ✅ Fits ICP (5-20 therapists, using M365)
- ✅ Has pain points (manual processes)
- ✅ Willing to provide feedback
- ✅ Committed to 90-day trial
- ✅ Can provide case study if successful

- **Access**: Full feature set
- **Support**: White-glove onboarding

**Target**: Small to mid-size clinics (5-20 therapists)

**Includes**:
- All 4 modules (Intake, Scheduling, Data Collection, Billing)
- Unlimited therapists and patients
- AI note generation
- Microsoft 365 integration
- Priority support
- Standard onboarding

**Early Adopter Discount**: $199/month (first 10 customers, 60% off)

#### Enterprise Plan - $999/month
**Target**: Multi-location clinics, larger practices

**Includes**:
- Everything in Professional
- Multi-location support
- Advanced features
- Dedicated support
- Custom integrations
- SLA guarantees

### Pricing Rationale

**Value-Based Pricing**:
- Average therapist salary: $50-70K/year
- Time saved: 20+ hours/month = $2,000+ value/month
- Our price: $499/month = 25% of value delivered
- **ROI**: 4:1 return on investment

**Competitive Analysis**:
- CentralReach: $300-500/month (no AI, complex)
- Motivity: $200-400/month (limited features)
- Our price: $499/month (AI-first, integrated)
- **Positioning**: Premium but justified by AI and integration

## 4. Sales Process & Playbook

### Sales Funnel

```
Awareness (1,000)
    ↓ 10% conversion
Interest (100)
    ↓ 20% conversion
Consideration (20)
    ↓ 25% conversion
Pilot (5)
    ↓ 80% conversion
Paying Customer (4)
```

### Stage 1: Awareness → Interest

**Channels**:
- LinkedIn content
- Blog posts
- Webinars
- Industry forums

**CTA**: "Request Demo" or "Try Free Pilot"

**Qualification**:
- Fits ICP (5-20 therapists)
- Using Microsoft 365
- Has pain points
- Budget available

### Stage 2: Interest → Consideration

**Activities**:
- Demo call (30 minutes)
- ROI calculator
- Case studies
- Pilot program offer

**Demo Script** (5 minutes):
1. Problem statement (2 min)
2. Solution overview (2 min)
3. Live demo (1 min)
4. Pilot offer

**Objections & Responses**:
- "We're happy with current system" → Show ROI, time savings
- "Too expensive" → Early adopter pricing, ROI proof
- "Not ready to switch" → Free pilot, no commitment
- "Security concerns" → HIPAA compliance, encryption

### Stage 3: Consideration → Pilot

**Activities**:
- Pilot agreement
- Onboarding kickoff
- Success metrics setup
- Weekly check-ins scheduled

**Pilot Agreement**:
- 90-day free trial
- Success metrics defined
- Feedback commitment
- Case study option

### Stage 4: Pilot → Paying Customer

**Activities**:
- ROI report presentation
- Success celebration
- Conversion offer
- Contract negotiation

**Conversion Tactics**:
- Show measurable ROI
- Early adopter pricing
- Success guarantee
- Reference customer incentives

## 10. Risk Mitigation

### Acquisition Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| **No Pilot Sign-ups** | High | Multiple channels, compelling offer, referrals |
| **Low Demo Conversion** | Medium | Better qualification, improved demo, case studies |
| **High CAC** | Medium | Focus on referrals, content marketing, partnerships |

### Conversion Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Low Pilot-to-Paid** | High | Intensive support, ROI proof, success guarantee |
| **Long Sales Cycle** | Medium | Streamlined process, early adopter pricing |
| **Price Objections** | Medium | ROI calculator, value demonstration, flexible pricing |

### Retention Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| **High Churn** | High | Customer success program, quick fixes, value delivery |
| **Low Engagement** | Medium | Training, check-ins, feature adoption campaigns |
| **Competitor Switching** | Medium | Deep integration, network effects, switching costs |

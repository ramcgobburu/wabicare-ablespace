# WabiCare 0-1 Product Roadmap: Executive Summary

**For**: Leadership, Investors, Board  
**Date**: January 2025  
**Timeline**: 12 Weeks to Product-Market Fit

---

## 🎯 The Big Picture

**Mission**: Launch integrated ABA practice management platform connecting Intake → Scheduling → Data Collection → Billing in one seamless workflow.

**Goal**: Acquire 3-5 paying customers in 90 days, proving:
- 64% reduction in intake time (2 weeks → 5 days)
- 80% reduction in scheduling conflicts
- 87% reduction in documentation time
- 19% improvement in claims approval (80% → 95%+)

---

## 📊 Current State vs. Target State

### Module Completion Status

| Module | Current | Target (0-1) | Gap |
|--------|--------|--------------|-----|
| **Scheduling** | 80% | 95% | Multi-user views, no-show tracking |
| **Billing** | 30% | 90% | Claims generation, clearinghouse |
| **Intake** | 40% | 90% | Workflow dashboard, e-signature |
| **Data Collection** | 20% | 90% | Offline PWA, templates, integration |

### Integration Status

**✅ Working**:
- Scheduling ↔ Authorization Tracking

**❌ Missing (Critical)**:
- Intake → Scheduling (assessment booking)
- Scheduling → Data Collection (session roster)
- Data Collection → Billing (claims generation)
- Intake → Billing (authorization)
